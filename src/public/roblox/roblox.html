Balanced Game

NEWGAM2, 2022 – 2023, passion project

a pvp combat game where you build your own moveset and use it against other players. i started it and led it past 11K visits, with some help from my friends.

one exploiter kept coming back. they used remote spy to watch what the client sent the server, then sent it fake data for an unfair edge. i spent a month down the rabbit hole of roblox exploits and anti-cheats, then made an anti-cheat and fixed the insecure networking.

this is where i built up my game dev skills: animating, VFX, building and programming.

i made these videos of it too, editing included.
