the discord bot

in grade 9 i made a discord chatbot. it started on the Chai API, moved to OpenAI's GPT models, and later got a bridge from JavaScript to Python so it could draw pictures with Hugging Face models. it eventually turned into Avy.

it's moved house a few times. first Replit, back when it was a free online IDE that just ran your server (before it became an AI website maker 😒). then Glitch, full of fun little projects and the cutest UI, until it shut down. now it lives on Koyeb.

keeping it alive through all that is how i got good at JavaScript before post secondary, which helped a lot once SAIT taught us TypeScript and React.
